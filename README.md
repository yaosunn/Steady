# Steady

A calm workout journal: an adaptive training plan, food and water targets, daily check-ins and progress history. English and 中文.

**Open it:** visit this site's GitHub Pages address on your phone, then use **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPhone, Safari) or **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (Android, Chrome).

## Your data

Everything you log is saved in your phone's browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. To move to a new phone, use **Settings → Export** to save a backup file, then **Import** it on the new phone.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-*.png`, `favicon.svg`: home-screen name and icon
- `sw.js`: lets the app open offline after the first visit
